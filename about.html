About

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This site is a free, zero-server Ziwei chart tool (PWA). Below are the open-source project, dataset and licences it uses.

Code

This site is built on the open-source project ziwei-doushu (Renhuai123/ziwei-doushu), provided under the MIT licence. The chart engine and the rest of this site's code are open source.

Interpretation dataset

The thirteen-dimension interpretations come from the open interpretation dataset v4.0 (674,976 charts × 13 dimensions), provided under CC BY 4.0. Attribution is required when reusing it.

Quotations from Ni Hai-Xia

The passages quoted from Ni Hai-Xia's Tian Ji in the interpretations are provided under CC BY-NC-SA, for non-commercial use only; please credit the source and the original episode when quoting them.

Entertainment only

All astrology content on this site is for entertainment and study only. It is not medical, investment, legal or counselling advice, nor advice for major life decisions.

Privacy

Birth data is processed only on your device and is never uploaded or sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy page for details.
